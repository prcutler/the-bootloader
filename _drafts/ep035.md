### Terry Godier (Paul #1)

In *A Room You Can’t See*, Terry asks: what has changed in how we listen to music - the technology or us? Without spoiling too much, Terry digs into what does it mean when we have an endless supply of music at our fingertips from streaming services for one monthly fee? Where is what, he calls, the intentionality, of what we listen to.  He asks himself when the last time he really listened to an album. Sat down and paid attention to it - not in the background when working or in the kitchen.

He even touches on the music being streamed on Spotify that is AI - and Terry says he’s not mad about the music, he’s mad that it even works and these songs get millions of listens.

Terry challenges you to think about your relationship with music in a video that is about 10 minutes long and really makes you think.

[A Room You Can’t See video essay](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GzzNBPqZhw0)

His second video is about design and it’s called “Some Decisions Aren’t Supposed to Be Optimized” - and he asks when did we hand over design decisions to the machine? He talks about how, for him, design is a feel. He tells a story about how a designer at Google quit back in 2009 because Google was testing, not one, not three, but 41 different shades of blue through A/B testing. He talks about how testing could be answering - and asking - the wrong questions.  It really makes you think into how and why decisions are made in your favorite products.

[Some Decisions Aren’t Supposed to be Optimized video essay](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-FrnyEggE4k
)

After watching his videos, I checked out his blog and realized I was using one of the products he created. Earlier this year, and I’ve been debating about sharing it on the show even, Terry released Current, an RSS reader for macOS, iOS and iPadOS.  Current has a really interesting goal - that your RSS feed shouldn’t feel like an inbox an dshow you the unread count. You should read your RSS feed for the joy of reading and stay in the flow while doing it. I switched to it earlier this year after buying it for $10, and I’ve been really happy with it. What a small world it is sometimes.

### AP30 Music Boy

The AP30 Music Boy is [live on Kickstarter]. Imagine an old Gameboy, but about half as tall - it looks like that, including the buttons.
It fits in the palm of your hand and they call it "Nostalgia you can actually here". It's an MP3 player that features 
Hi-Res Audio certification, a 3.5mm jack for headphones, as well as Bluetooth.

You can back one of two different editions on Kickstarter starting at about $85 for the plastic case or $93 for one in an aluminum case.
